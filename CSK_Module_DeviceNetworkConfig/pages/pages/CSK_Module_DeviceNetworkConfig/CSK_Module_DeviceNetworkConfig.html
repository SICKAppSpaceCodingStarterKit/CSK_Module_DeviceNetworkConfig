There might be other possiblities (e.g. OS or ControlCenter). To delete a nameserver, please select a DNS entry from the list and press 'Remove'.
A maximum of three nameserves can be added. Nameservers that are added automatically via DHCP cannot be deleted.
Please note that the DNS entries can only be persistent, if the app 'CSK_Module_PersistentData' is loaded. Important: With a wrong setup this might make your device unaccessable. So test your setup first with a limited time period! It can take about a minute to activate the new bridge setup. Please login with at least user level 'Maintenance' or higher to see this page.
